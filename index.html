Profile

I'm an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Inha University, advised by Younggun Cho in SPARO Lab. I also received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering from Inha University. During my M.S., I worked on learning-based state estimation, focusing on learned inertial odometry, and I'm now studying robot motion for robust and adaptive behavior. For my personal thoughts and research experiences, please check out my :)
