Hi, I'm Jiwon.

Learning-based state estimation & proprioceptive odometry

I'm an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Inha University, advised by Younggun Cho in SPARO Lab, where I also received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering. During my M.S. I worked on self-supervised inertial odometry; I'm now working on proprioceptive state estimation — modeling and correcting environment-induced error from inertial, kinematic, and contact signals alone.
